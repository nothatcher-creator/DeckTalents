# Legendary Talents

Each Legendary costs 10 Talent Points and requires the deck's original Ultimate plus late-tree upgrades.

## Red Deck — Scarlet Apocalypse

With no discards remaining, every hand gains +150 Mult and X3.5 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `final_cut, seeing_red`

## Blue Deck — Azure Infinity

While at least 4 hands remain, gain +75 Chips per remaining hand and X1.75 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `endless_shift, ocean_of_hands`

## Yellow Deck — Golden Singularity

Boss victories award $25; at $100 or more, every hand gains X3.5 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `money_printer, midas_boss`

## Green Deck — Planet Saver

Gain X0.35 Mult per remaining hand and discard, and earn $2 per unused resource at cashout.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `perpetual_motion, green_machine`

## Black Deck — Abyssal Crown

Gain +2 Joker slots; with 5 or fewer Jokers, gain +20 Mult per Joker and X3.25 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `singularity_storage, black_hole`

## Magic Deck — Impossible Act

Holding all 3 consumable types grants +100 Chips per held consumable and X3.5 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `master_magician, grand_illusion`

## Nebula Deck — Heat Death

Level 8 or higher hands gain +100 Chips per level and X3.25 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `galaxy_brain, supernova`

## Ghost Deck — Beyond the Veil

While holding a Spectral card, gain +75 Chips per enhanced card and X3.25 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `mass_haunting, paranormal_activity`

## Abandoned Deck — Lord of the Flies

Five numbered cards gain +150 Mult and X3.25 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `kids_rule, empty_nest`

## Checkered Deck — Perfect Checkmate

Flushes gain +100 Mult and X3.25 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `grand_pattern, full_board`

## Zodiac Deck — Grand Zodiac

Holding all 3 types grants +150 Chips per type and X3.5 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `fate_written, big_three`

## Painted Deck — Magnum Opus

At hand size 9+, gain +25 Mult per card remaining and X3.25 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `museum_piece, masterpiece`

## Anaglyph Deck — Printing Press

Boss victories create 3 Double Tags, and every Tag grants X0.4 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `infinite_labels, echo_chamber`

## Plasma Deck — Star Core

Every played hand gains +500 Chips, +100 Mult, and X3 Mult.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `plasma_storm, fusion_reactor`

## Erratic Deck — Absolute Pandemonium

Every hand gains random Chips, Mult, and X3 to X6 Mult; Bosses have a 25% chance to award an extra Talent Point.

Cost: **10 points**  
Requires: `house_always_loses, rngesus`
