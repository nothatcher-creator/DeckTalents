# Global Talent List

All global talents work with every deck and use the same Talent Points earned from Boss Blind victories.

## Optional cosmetics

1. **Royal Entrance** — Boss Blinds enter with a dramatic purple announcement.
2. **Small Fry** — Small Blinds receive a tiny but confident introduction.
3. **Big Mood** — Big Blinds receive an unnecessarily serious introduction.
4. **First-Hand Fireworks** — The first hand each Blind receives a flashy announcement.
5. **Discard Drama** — The first discard each Blind is treated like a major event.
6. **Tarot Sparkles** — Using a Tarot card produces a magical announcement.
7. **Planetary Ping** — Using a Planet card produces a spacey announcement.
8. **Ghostly Echo** — Using a Spectral card produces a spooky announcement.
9. **Five-Card Salute** — The first five-card hand each Blind receives a salute.
10. **Victory Lap** — Winning a Blind triggers a celebratory announcement.

Owned cosmetics can be toggled on or off by clicking them again.

## Universal gameplay talents

1. **Pocket Change** — +$1 at every cashout.
2. **Rainy Day Fund** — Another +$2 at cashout while below $10.
3. **Compound Training** — Another +$2 at cashout while at $25 or more.
4. **Golden Parachute** — Boss victories pay $8; at $50 or more, gain X1.4 Mult.
5. **Bigger Pockets** — +1 consumable slot.
6. **Joker Annex** — +1 Joker slot.
7. **Wide Grip** — +1 hand size.
8. **Full House Renovation** — Another +1 Joker slot, consumable slot, and hand size.
9. **Plan B** — +1 discard every Blind.
10. **Extra Shift** — +1 hand every Blind.
11. **Chip Training** — Every hand gains +30 Chips.
12. **Mult Training** — Every hand gains +6 Mult.
13. **First Impressions** — First hand each Blind gains +75 Chips and +12 Mult.
14. **The Closer** — Final available hand gains +25 Mult.
15. **Joker Union** — +4 Mult per owned Joker.
16. **Arcane Resonance** — +20 Chips per held consumable.
17. **Momentum** — Hands after the first gain +15 Mult.
18. **Boss Slayer** — Hands against Boss Blinds gain X2 Mult.
19. **Never Say Die** — Final available hand gains X2.5 Mult.
20. **Ascension** — Every played hand gains X1.5 Mult with every deck.
